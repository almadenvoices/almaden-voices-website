We Made the Front Page!

 

I’m incredibly excited to share that Almaden Voices was just featured as a front-page cover story in the Almaden Times!

This is such a special milestone for us, and it truly would not have happened without the support of our community — whether you’ve donated, registered for a workshop, attended one of our sessions, or simply followed along and cheered us on.

And speaking of exciting things, our recent 1-on-1 public speaking coaching sessions sold out almost immediately! If you missed them, keep an eye out — I’m hoping to open more opportunities soon. In the meantime, join the waitlist at almadenvoices.org/register

We’re Looking for Volunteers!

 

Almaden Voices has officially opened 9 volunteer positions, with opportunities ranging from grants research and community outreach to newsletter writing and website development.

There’s a role for all kinds of interests and skills. Every role except Events Coordinator is remote, and the Instructor role has both online and in-person opportunities.

And these roles aren’t just for students — we’d love parent volunteers too! If you’ve been looking for a way to get involved with Almaden Voices yourself, there’s a place for you here as well.

If you’re interested in getting involved, you can learn more and apply here:

almadenvoices.org/volunteer

Volunteers must be in 8th grade or higher, and applications are currently open until September 4th. If you’re interested but aren’t able to apply by the deadline, please reach out to me — I’d still love to see if we can find a way for you to get involved!

Deadline

Applications close Friday, September 4.
