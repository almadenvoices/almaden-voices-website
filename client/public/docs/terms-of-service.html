1. About Almaden Voices

Almaden Voices is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering young voices through free and low-cost speech, debate, and communication skills programs for students ages 5 through 15 in our community.

2. Eligibility and Registration

Registration must be completed by a parent or legal guardian on behalf of the student. By registering a student, the parent or guardian confirms that all information provided is accurate and complete.

Registration does not guarantee enrollment. Almaden Voices reserves the right to limit class sizes and manage enrollment at its discretion. We will contact registrants within 2 to 3 business days of registration with session details and schedule.

3. Program Participation

Sessions typically run for 4 to 10 weeks depending on the program level. Programs include Public Speaking, Debate, Leadership Workshops, Storytelling, and Communication Skills. Sessions may include a final showcase event where students present in front of an audience of parents and community members.

Almaden Voices reserves the right to modify session schedules, content, locations, or formats at any time. Registered families will be notified of any changes. Students are expected to participate respectfully. Almaden Voices reserves the right to remove any participant whose behavior disrupts the program.

Where that permission has been given, the photos and videos may be used on our website, program materials, and social media pages. We identify students by first name only and never publish full names, schools, or contact details.

You may withdraw that permission at any time, or ask us to remove a photo or video, by emailing us at almadenvoices@gmail.com, and we will remove it within 30 days.
