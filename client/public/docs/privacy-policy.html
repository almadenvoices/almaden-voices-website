Privacy Policy

Almaden Voices

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

Almaden Voices is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that provides free speech, debate, and communication skills programs to students ages 5 through 14. We are committed to protecting the privacy of the children and families we serve.

Contact: Almaden Voices, a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation
Email: almadenvoices@gmail.com | Website: almadenvoices.org

2. Information We Collect

When a parent or guardian registers a student for a program or event, we collect:

Parent/Guardian Information:

  • Parent or guardian full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing address (only if required for the specific program)

Student Information:

  • First and last name
  • Grade level
  • Program or event enrolled in

We collect only the information necessary to operate our programs. We do not collect financial information — any donations are processed by PayPal and are not stored by Almaden Voices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use registration information only for the following purposes:

  • To enroll students in programs and confirm registrations
  • To communicate with families about session details, schedules, and updates
  • To contact you about future Almaden Voices sessions and events, and to send you our newsletter (only if you checked the Future Contact box on the registration form)
  • To contact you in case of schedule changes or emergencies
  • To improve our programs

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

4. Children's Privacy — COPPA Standards

Almaden Voices serves students ages 5 through 14, many of whom are under 13 years of age. As a nonprofit, we voluntarily follow the standards set out in the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and we take the following measures:

  • All registrations are completed by a parent or legal guardian
  • We collect student information only from the parent or legal guardian, never directly from the child, and the parent confirms consent by completing the registration form
  • We do not require children to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary to participate in our programs
  • Parents may review, update, or request deletion of their child's information at any time (see Section 8)

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child without proper parental consent, please contact us immediately at almadenvoices@gmail.com.

5. Photos and Video

We photograph and record students during sessions and events only where a parent or guardian has given permission on the registration form. Where that permission has been given, we may use the photos and videos on our website, program materials, and social media pages. We identify students by first name only, and we never publish full names, schools, or contact details alongside a photo or video.

You may withdraw that permission at any time by emailing almadenvoices@gmail.com. If you would like a photo or video removed, contact us at the same address. We will remove it from our materials within 30 days.

6. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Registration data is stored in a password-protected Google account in a restricted-access Google Sheets document. Access is limited to authorized Almaden Voices staff only.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, including:

  • Limiting access to authorized individuals only
  • Using two-factor authentication on all accounts that store personal data
  • Not sharing registration data via unencrypted email
  • Regularly reviewing who has access to stored data

Almaden Voices maintains a written information security program covering personal information collected from families. A designated member of our team coordinates the program, we assess risks to the information we hold at least annually, and we review and update our safeguards at least once a year.

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected families within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

7. Data Retention

We do not retain children's personal information indefinitely.

We distinguish between two types of data:

Operational data (emergency contacts, mailing address, session-specific details) is reviewed after each session and deleted once it is no longer needed for that program.

Contact data (parent name, email, phone) and student data (first and last name, grade level, program enrolled in) are deleted 10 years after a family's last program participation, unless the parent has asked to remain on our list for future sessions. We retain this information because Almaden Voices supports students across their school years, and families frequently return for later programs or enroll younger siblings.

Parents may request deletion of any or all of their information at any time by emailing almadenvoices@gmail.com with the word 'Privacy' in the subject line. We will complete all deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Your Rights — COPPA and CCPA

As a parent or guardian, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you or your child
  • Right to Correct: You may request that we correct any inaccurate information
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we permanently delete your information and/or your child's information from our records
  • Right to Withdraw Photo Permission: You may withdraw photo and video permission at any time, or ask us to remove an existing photo or video
  • Right to Opt Out of Communications: You may unsubscribe from non-essential communications at any time

California Residents (CCPA): Although Almaden Voices is a nonprofit and is generally not subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act, we extend the following rights to all families: the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion of their data, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at almadenvoices@gmail.com.

To exercise any of the above rights, email almadenvoices@gmail.com with the word 'Privacy' in your subject line. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Almaden Voices grows. When we make material changes, we will notify registered families by email and update the 'Last Updated' date at the top of this page.
