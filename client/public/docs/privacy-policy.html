Privacy Policy

Almaden Voices

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Who We Are

Almaden Voices is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that provides speech, debate, and communication skills programs to students ages 5 through 14. Our group workshops are free; one-on-one coaching and some programs are paid. We are committed to protecting the privacy of the children and families we serve.

Contact: Almaden Voices, a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation
Email: almadenvoices@gmail.com | Website: almadenvoices.org

2. Information We Collect

When you register a student for a program or event through AniSetu (see Section 3), we receive:

Parent/Guardian Information:

For a student aged 13 or over who registers themselves, this is the student's own contact information.

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

Student Information:

  • First and last name
  • Grade level
  • Program or event enrolled in

Information You Give Us Directly on almadenvoices.org:

  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address
  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number, country, and your message
  • Volunteer applications: the applicant's name, email address, phone number, age or grade, location, résumé (if attached), the roles applied for, availability, and answers to our questions. For applicants under 18, we also collect the names, email addresses, and phone numbers of two parents or guardians, so that we can always reach a parent in an emergency
  • One-on-one coaching bookings and the coaching waitlist: the parent's name, email address, and phone number; the student's name, age, and school; home ZIP code; what the student would like to work on; and photo and video permission. Payment is made through PayPal; we receive the amount paid and a PayPal order number, never card details

We collect only the information necessary to operate our programs. We do not collect financial information — any donations are processed by PayPal and are not stored by Almaden Voices.

3. Registration through AniSetu

Workshop registration for Almaden Voices is handled through AniSetu, an online platform operated by AniSetu, Inc. When you register for one of our programs, you do so on AniSetu's system rather than on almadenvoices.org.

What AniSetu collects. Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and grade level. If you are a parent registering a child, AniSetu also collects your child's name, date of birth and grade level. AniSetu collects payment information through Stripe for any paid program; Almaden Voices never sees card numbers. AniSetu also automatically collects technical information such as IP address and device type, and makes masked recordings of how signed-in users move through parts of its site. Their full practices are in their privacy policy at anisetu.com/privacy, which we encourage you to read.

What we receive. Almaden Voices receives the registration details for students enrolled in our programs, so that we can run the session, contact you about it, and know who to expect.

Who creates the account. AniSetu requires a parent or legal guardian to create the account and complete enrollment for any child under 13. Students aged 13 and over may create their own accounts on AniSetu with a parent's awareness. For our own records and communications, we ask that a parent or guardian be the point of contact for every student under 18.

Photo and video permission. Permission to photograph or record your child is collected at registration.

A relationship we want you to know about. AniSetu, Inc. was founded by Amit Bansal, who is also a member of the Almaden Voices board of directors. Almaden Voices pays AniSetu's standard platform fee. We mention this because families should know how the organizations handling their information are connected.

Deleting your information. If you ask us to delete your family's information, we delete it from our records within 30 days and request deletion from AniSetu at the same time. You can also request deletion from AniSetu directly at support@anisetu.com. Email us at almadenvoices@gmail.com.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use registration information only for the following purposes:

  • To enroll students in programs (AniSetu sends the confirmation when you register)
  • To communicate with families about session details, schedules, and updates
  • To contact you about future Almaden Voices sessions and events, and to send you our newsletter (only if you have signed up for our newsletter on almadenvoices.org)
  • To contact you in case of schedule changes or emergencies
  • To improve our programs

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Registration information is shared only with AniSetu, which handles registration on our behalf (see Section 3).

5. Children's Privacy — COPPA Standards

Almaden Voices serves students ages 5 through 14, many of whom are under 13 years of age. As a nonprofit, we voluntarily follow the standards set out in the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and we take the following measures:

  • For children under 13, registration is completed by a parent or legal guardian; neither we nor AniSetu permit a child under 13 to create an account. Students aged 13 and over may register themselves on AniSetu. We ask that a parent or guardian be our point of contact for every student under 18.
  • We do not require children to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary to participate in our programs
  • Parents may review, update, or request deletion of their child's information at any time (see Section 9)

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child without proper parental consent, please contact us immediately at almadenvoices@gmail.com.

6. Photos and Video

We photograph and record students during sessions and events only where a parent or guardian has given permission on registration. Where that permission has been given, we may use the photos and videos on our website, program materials, and social media pages. We identify students by first name only, and we never publish full names, schools, or contact details alongside a photo or video.

You may withdraw that permission at any time by emailing almadenvoices@gmail.com. If you would like a photo or video removed, contact us at the same address. We will remove it from our materials within 30 days.

7. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Registration data is held by AniSetu under their privacy policy. Any copy we keep is stored in a password-protected, restricted-access Google account. Access is limited to authorized Almaden Voices staff only.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, including:

  • Limiting access to authorized individuals only
  • Using two-factor authentication on all accounts that store personal data
  • Not sharing registration data via unencrypted email
  • Regularly reviewing who has access to stored data

Where a third party processes information on our behalf, we ask them to protect it to the same standard described here.

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected families within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

8. Data Retention

We do not retain children's personal information indefinitely.

We distinguish between two types of data:

Operational data (session-specific details, such as attendance and notes for a particular program) is reviewed after each session and deleted once it is no longer needed for that program.

Contact data (parent name, email, phone) and student data (first and last name, grade level, program enrolled in) are deleted 10 years after a family's last program participation, unless the parent has asked to remain on our list for future sessions. We retain this information because Almaden Voices supports students across their school years, and families frequently return for later programs or enroll younger siblings.

Parents may request deletion of any or all of their information at any time by emailing almadenvoices@gmail.com with the word 'Privacy' in the subject line. We will complete all deletion requests within 30 days.

Information held by AniSetu on our behalf is subject to AniSetu's retention schedule, described in their privacy policy. When we delete your information at your request, we ask AniSetu to delete their copy at the same time.

9. Your Rights — COPPA and CCPA

As a parent or guardian, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you or your child
  • Right to Correct: You may request that we correct any inaccurate information
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we permanently delete your information and/or your child's information from our records
  • Right to Withdraw Photo Permission: You may withdraw photo and video permission at any time, or ask us to remove an existing photo or video
  • Right to Opt Out of Communications: You may unsubscribe from non-essential communications at any time

California Residents (CCPA): Although Almaden Voices is a nonprofit and is generally not subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act, we extend the following rights to all families: the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion of their data, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at almadenvoices@gmail.com.

To exercise any of the above rights, email almadenvoices@gmail.com with the word 'Privacy' in your subject line. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Coaching Payments

Coaching payments are processed by PayPal. We do not receive or store card or payment details. Payment for a coaching spot that is never confirmed, or that is confirmed but goes unused, is treated as a donation to Almaden Voices rather than refunded automatically. If your circumstances change, or you believe a charge was made in error, please contact us at almadenvoices@gmail.com and we will review your request individually. Our full payment terms are set out in our Terms of Service.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Almaden Voices grows. When we make material changes, we will notify registered families by email and update the 'Last Updated' date at the top of this page.
